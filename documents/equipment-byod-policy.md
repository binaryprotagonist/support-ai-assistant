# Company Equipment and BYOD Security Policy

## 1. Laptop Provisioning and Refresh Cadence
Every engineering and corporate employee is issued a standard company-managed workstation (Apple MacBook Pro or Dell Precision). Hardware is scheduled for a mandatory hardware refresh cycle every 36 months of active service.

## 2. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Mobile Rules
Employees utilizing personal smartphones or tablets to access corporate email, Slack, or Google Workspace must enroll their device in the corporate Mobile Device Management (MDM) profile, requiring a minimum 6-digit passcode and biometric unlock.

## 3. Immediate Lost or Stolen Equipment Reporting
If any company-issued device or personal device holding corporate data is lost, misplaced, or stolen, the employee must notify the IT Security Hotline (security@company.com) within 2 hours of discovery so a remote wipe command can be initiated.

## 4. Prohibited Software and Unapproved AI Tools
Installing unauthorized third-party software, peer-to-peer torrent clients, or uploading proprietary customer code or confidential financial documents to unvetted public AI platforms without written approval from InfoSec is strictly prohibited.
