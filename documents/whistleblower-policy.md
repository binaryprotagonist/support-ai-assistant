# Corporate Whistleblower and Anti-Retaliation Policy

## 1. Scope and Covered Misconduct
This policy protects employees, contractors, and third parties who report suspected illegal activities, accounting fraud, bribery, severe environmental safety breaches, or gross ethical misconduct within the organization.

## 2. Anonymous Whistleblower Hotline
Reports can be submitted anonymously 24/7 through the independent whistleblower hotline (1-800-555-ETHIC) or via the encrypted external web portal at ethics.company-whistleblower.com.

## 3. Strict Non-Retaliation Guarantee
The company strictly prohibits any form of retaliation, adverse employment action, harassment, demotion, or discrimination against any individual who submits a report in good faith. Retaliation constitutes severe misconduct resulting in immediate dismissal.

## 4. Investigation Cadence and Reporting
All complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and investigated by an independent compliance committee within 30 business days. Findings and remedial actions are reported directly to the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors.
