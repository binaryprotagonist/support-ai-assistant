# Remote Work and Hybrid Workplace Policy

## 1. Hybrid Work Schedule
The company operates on a flexible hybrid model. Full-time corporate employees are expected to work from an authorized company office a minimum of 2 days per week, with up to 3 days permitted as remote work from an approved home location.

## 2. Core Working Hours
To facilitate cross-timezone collaboration, all employees regardless of remote location must be available for synchronous communication and team meetings during core working hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local office time.

## 3. Home Office Ergonomic Stipend
Upon completion of the 90-day probationary period, newly hired full-time remote or hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time, non-taxable home office setup stipend of up to $800 to purchase ergonomic furniture, external monitors, or peripherals.

## 4. High-Speed Internet Reimbursement
Employees working remotely at least 2 days per week are eligible to claim a monthly internet reimbursement of up to $60 through the expense management portal, accompanied by an itemized ISP utility invoice.

## 5. Temporary International Remote Work ("Work from Anywhere")
Employees in good standing may request to work remotely from an approved international country for a maximum of 30 business days per calendar year. Requests must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance to evaluate legal, tax, and cyber compliance.
