# Code of Business Conduct and Ethics Policy

## 1. Anti-Harassment and Zero-Tolerance Commitment
The company maintains an unconditional zero-tolerance stance against harassment, bullying, sexual misconduct, and discrimination based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or age.

## 2. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must avoid outside activities or personal financial relationships that conflict with company obligations. Any secondary employment, advisory board seats, or significant equity stakes in corporate suppliers or competitors must be disclosed and approved by Legal.

## 3. Gifts, Meals, and Corporate Hospitality
Employees may not accept gifts, entertainment, or meals from suppliers or clients with a fair market value exceeding $100 USD. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (such as pre-paid gift cards) are strictly prohibited regardless of amount.

## 4. Protection and Proper Use of Corporate Assets
Company property, intellectual property, proprietary software code, and confidential commercial data must be safeguarded diligently and used solely for legitimate business operations.
