# Employee Health and Wellness Benefits Policy

## 1. Medical, Dental, and Vision Coverage
The company offers comprehensive health insurance plans to all full-time regular employees and eligible dependents starting on the first day of the month following the hire date. The employer covers 85% of employee premiums and 70% of dependent premiums for standard PPO and HDHP tiers.

## 2. 401(k) Retirement Contribution Matching
Employees may enroll in the corporate 401(k) retirement plan immediately upon hire. The company provides a 100% dollar-for-dollar match on employee elective deferrals up to the first 4% of eligible base salary, plus a 50% match on the next 2% of salary, vesting immediately.

## 3. Annual Wellness and Fitness Subsidy
To encourage physical and mental well-being, employees may claim up to $750 USD annually for qualified fitness expenses, including gym memberships, fitness trackers, yoga studio classes, and ergonomic wellness subscriptions.

## 4. Professional Learning and Tuition Support
Each employee has an annual professional development budget of $1,500 USD for approved job-related certifications, conferences, textbooks, and technical training courses, subject to departmental manager approval.

## 5. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
The company provides a confidential Employee Assistance Program available 24/7/365, offering up to 6 free counseling sessions per year per life event for employees and household members dealing with stress, grief, or financial planning.
