# Corporate Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Flight Bookings and Travel Class
All domestic and continental flights under 6 hours in total duration must be booked in Standard Economy class. For international transcontinental flights exceeding 6 continuous hours of flight time, Premium Economy or Business Class bookings are permitted with prior VP-level written approval.

## 2. Daily Meal Allowances (Per Diem)
When traveling on authorized company business, employees are provided a daily meal per diem cap of $85 USD in major metropolitan hubs (New York, London, Tokyo, San Francisco) and $65 USD in other locations. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner.

## 3. Hotel Accommodations
Hotels must be reserved through the corporate travel concierge tool. The nightly room rate cap is $220 USD excluding local occupancy taxes. Bookings exceeding this cap require direct Director pre-approval prior to trip confirmation.

## 4. Expense Submission Window and Receipts
All business expense claims must be filed within 30 calendar days following the completion of the business trip. Itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual expense equal to or exceeding $25 USD. Credit card summary slips without itemized breakdowns will be rejected.

## 5. Ground Transportation
Employees are encouraged to utilize public transit or standard rideshare services (UberX / Lyft Standard). Rental cars are only authorized when multiple remote client locations must be visited or when public transit is unavailable. Luxury car upgrades will not be reimbursed.
