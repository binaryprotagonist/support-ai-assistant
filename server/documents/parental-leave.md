# Parental and Family Leave Policy

## 1. Primary Caregiver Paid Leave
All eligible full-time employees welcoming a new child through birth, legal adoption, or surrogacy are entitled to 16 weeks of 100% fully paid primary caregiver leave. This leave may be taken consecutively or intermittently within 12 months of the child's arrival.

## 2. Secondary Caregiver Paid Leave
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 8 weeks of 100% fully paid leave to bond with their new child, usable within the first 12 months following birth or adoptive placement.

## 3. Gradual Return-to-Work Ramp
Upon returning from parental leave, employees may elect a 4-week transition ramp schedule, working 80% of normal hours (4 days per week) while receiving 100% of their base compensation to ease family integration.

## 4. Adoption and Fertility Assistance Reimbursement
The company offers lifetime financial assistance up to $10,000 USD per family towards eligible expenses incurred during legal adoption proceedings, surrogacy agency fees, or fertility treatments (such as IVF or egg freezing).
