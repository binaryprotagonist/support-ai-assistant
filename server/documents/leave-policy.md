# Leave and Time-Off Policy

## 1. Annual Paid Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per completed month of service. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on contracted working hours.

## 2. Leave Request and Advance Notice
All annual leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days must be submitted via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance and require approval from the employee's direct line manager. Requests for single-day leaves should be submitted at least 48 hours prior.

## 3. Leave Carryover and Expiry
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the subsequent calendar year. Any carried-over days must be utilized before March 31st of the new year, after which they will automatically expire without financial compensation.

## 4. Sick Leave and Medical Certification
Employees are granted up to 10 paid sick leave days per year. If sick leave extends beyond 3 consecutive business days, the employee is required to present a valid medical certificate from a licensed physician upon their return to work.

## 5. Bereavement and Compassionate Leave
Employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive paid business days of compassionate leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). For extended relatives, 2 days of paid leave are permitted.

## 6. Public Holidays
The company observes 10 designated statutory public holidays each year. When a recognized holiday falls on a weekend, either the preceding Friday or the following Monday will be designated as the observed day off.
