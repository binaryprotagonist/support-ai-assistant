# Employee Separation, Offboarding, and Severance Policy

## 1. Voluntary Resignation Notice Periods
Individual contributors are requested to provide a minimum of 2 weeks written notice prior to departure. Team leads, managers, and directors are contractually required to provide 4 weeks notice to ensure structured handover.

## 2. Severance Pay Formula
In the event of an involuntary company layoff or restructuring, full-time employees receive statutory severance of 2 weeks base pay per full year of continuous service, with a guaranteed minimum severance payout of 4 weeks pay.

## 3. Equipment Return Deadline
All company property, including laptops, monitors, access keycards, and peripheral hardware, must be returned to the office or shipped via prepaid corporate courier within 5 business days following the last official day of employment.

## 4. Garden Leave Provisions
At the company's discretion, an employee serving out a notice period may be placed on paid Garden Leave, during which they remain employed and receive full salary but are relieved of daily duties and must not access corporate networks.
