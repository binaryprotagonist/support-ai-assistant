# Cybersecurity Incident Response Policy

## 1. Incident Severity Levels
Security incidents are classified into four severity tiers: SEV-1 (Critical: confirmed breach of customer data or ransomware outage), SEV-2 (High: compromised admin credential or unauthorized production access), SEV-3 (Moderate: phishing attempt targeting employees or isolated malware), and SEV-4 (Low: minor policy deviation or informational alert).

## 2. Mandatory Reporting Timeframes
Any employee or contractor discovering evidence of a potential security vulnerability, credential compromise, or phishing campaign must report it to security-response@company.com within 60 minutes of detection.

## 3. Incident Commander and Response Team (CIRT)
During a SEV-1 or SEV-2 incident, an Incident Commander from the Cybersecurity Incident Response Team assumes operational control, conducts forensic triage, isolates affected network segments, and coordinates customer notifications.

## 4. Post-Incident Review (PIR)
Within 5 business days following resolution of a SEV-1 or SEV-2 incident, a formal Post-Incident Review must be conducted, documented, and presented to the Executive Leadership team with preventative action items.
