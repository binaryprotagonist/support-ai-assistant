# Data Privacy and Information Security Policy

## 1. Personally Identifiable Information (PII) Handling
All customer and employee Personally Identifiable Information (PII) must be encrypted both in transit (using TLS 1.3 or higher) and at rest (using AES-256 encryption). Production data must never be copied to local machines or non-production test databases without full pseudonymization.

## 2. Security Breach Notification SLA
In the event of a confirmed or suspected security incident involving customer personal data, the IT Security and Legal teams must report the breach to relevant regulatory authorities within 72 hours of becoming aware, as mandated by GDPR.

## 3. Data Retention and Scheduled Purging
Customer transaction logs and interaction histories are retained for a maximum of 7 years in accordance with tax compliance. User marketing interaction data must be automatically purged after 24 months of account inactivity.

## 4. Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR)
Customer requests for data export, deletion, or correction under GDPR or CCPA must be forwarded to privacy@company.com and completed within 30 calendar days of verified identity confirmation.
