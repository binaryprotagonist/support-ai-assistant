# Employee Performance Review and Promotion Policy

## 1. Bi-Annual Performance Review Cycles
Formal performance evaluations are conducted twice per year: the Mid-Year Review cycle in July and the Year-End Review cycle in January. Employees and managers complete self-evaluations and 360-degree peer feedback reviews.

## 2. Performance Rating System
Performance is assessed against five standard tiers: Exceeds Expectations (Level 5), Consistently Meets Expectations (Level 4), Meets Expectations (Level 3), Needs Improvement (Level 2), and Unsatisfactory (Level 1).

## 3. Promotion Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for formal promotion to the next career band, an employee must have maintained a minimum rating of Level 4 or higher across at least two consecutive performance cycles and demonstrated sustained execution at the next target level for a minimum of 6 months.

## 4. Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
Employees receiving a rating of Level 2 or Level 1 are placed on a structured 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with weekly milestone check-ins with HR and the department manager. Failure to meet PIP targets may result in contract termination.
